CryptoAnaliz helps you follow the crypto markets with live charts, technical indicator signals and AI-written analyses, all in one simple app.

LIVE CHARTS
• Live candlestick charts with public Binance market data
• Spot and futures pairs
• Timeframes from 1 minute to 1 week

BUY / SELL SIGNALS
• Signals calculated from technical indicators such as RSI and Bollinger Bands
• Signals are shown on the chart in three strength levels

RADAR
• Scans the market and lists coins with notable technical movements

AI ANALYSIS
• Quick analysis: a short summary of the technical data
• Deep analysis: combines the technical data with recent news headlines about the coin
• Analyses use credits; credits are only deducted when an analysis completes successfully
• Review your past analyses at any time

WATCHLIST AND NOTIFICATIONS
• Add coins to your watchlist and get notified when a new signal appears

PRICE ALERTS (PRO)
• Set a target price and get a notification when the price reaches it

CREDITS AND PRO SUBSCRIPTION
• Free package: 10 credits every month (welcome credit after e-mail verification)
• Pro: monthly subscription (price shown in Google Play); 300 credits every month and Pro features such as price alerts
• A quick analysis uses 2 credits and a deep analysis uses 5; one-time credit packs are available through Google Play
• Monthly credits do not carry over: on your renewal day the monthly credits left in your account are completed to the package amount and unused ones expire. Purchased credits never expire; monthly credits are spent first
• Free members whose credits do not cover a quick analysis can watch an optional rewarded ad to run a quick analysis only

LANGUAGES
• English, Turkish and Spanish

PRIVACY
• Free members see Google AdMob ads; Pro members see no ads
• Anonymous crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are collected for stability
• Delete your account at any time from inside the app
• Privacy policy: https://www.cryptoanaliz.net/privacy
